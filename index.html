Signs it’s time for a cleaning

Florida’s heat and humidity keep AC systems running most of the year, so dust and lint build up quickly. Here are a few common signs to watch for.

Air ducts & HVAC

  • Dust blowing from the vents, or settling again soon after you clean
  • A musty smell when the AC turns on
  • Dust or debris around the supply registers and return grilles
  • Allergies that seem worse at home
  • A recent renovation, or you just moved in

Dryer vent

  • Clothes need more than one cycle to dry
  • The dryer or laundry room feels unusually hot
  • A burning smell while the dryer runs
  • Lint collecting around the outside vent flap

How often should I have my air ducts cleaned?

Many homes benefit from duct cleaning every 3–5 years. You may want it sooner if you have pets, allergies or asthma in the home, recent renovations, or you notice extra dust, musty odors, or debris coming from your vents.

What’s the difference between duct cleaning and HVAC restoration?

Duct cleaning focuses on removing dust and debris from the ductwork that carries air through your home. HVAC restoration goes further, deep cleaning the system itself, like the blower and air handler, so it can move air efficiently and perform at its best.

How do I know if my dryer vent needs cleaning?

Common signs include clothes taking longer than usual to dry, the dryer or laundry room feeling unusually hot, a burning smell, or lint collecting around the outside vent flap. A clogged vent makes your dryer work harder and is a leading cause of dryer fires.
